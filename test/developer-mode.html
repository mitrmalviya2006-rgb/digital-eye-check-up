Manual checks requiring real user/device

  1. Use a supported HTTPS or localhost browser and grant camera permission when prompted.
  2. Confirm the active camera is rear-facing if your device exposes one; verify any camera fallback is clearly reported.
  3. Check torch only if the camera reports support. Do not infer flash support from the device model.
  4. Follow the real screening flow to check positioning, both-eye detection, image quality, and auto-capture. Eye/face model artifacts must actually load.
  5. Do not use generated test reports as clinical evidence. With no configured trained refractive-error model, Component 9 and downstream prediction/result stages cannot pass end-to-end validation.
